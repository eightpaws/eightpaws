# Anton Korovin · IT Director

20 years in IT, 18 of them in management. I currently own the IT landscape of a legal entity inside the Bank of Russia structure: 2,000 users, a RUB 1.5 bn budget, procurement under 44-FZ, and technical decisions agreed with the regulator.

**What I publish here.** Not tutorial projects and not forks, but measurements I ran myself: numbers with the rig, the commands and the raw data behind them. Negative results are published next to positive ones, because they save time for whoever comes next.

- Infrastructure inside an isolated perimeter: virtualisation, storage, high availability, backups with restore verification.
- Import substitution without downtime: localisation after vendors left, zero interruptions to business services.
- Local AI on hardware I own: rig, models, measurements, business case, with data never leaving the perimeter.
- Budget and procurement: OpEx and CapEx structure, justification to management, savings under 44-FZ.
